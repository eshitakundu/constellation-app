About this little sky

Your Constellation is a creative coding experiment: a way to turn ordinary text into a small piece of personal star art. Enter a name or a phrase, watch its stars connect, and save the result as an image.

Art from a simple rule

Each character contributes a position and brightness to the pattern. A minimum spanning tree connects the points into a single structure. This combination of a fixed rule and a personal input is the heart of the project: you choose the words, and the algorithm gives them a visual form.

The result is an imagined sky. It is not an astronomical chart, an astrological reading, or a scientifically unique fingerprint. Different inputs can sometimes create similar or overlapping patterns.

Two skies, one collision

Add another name to reveal two constellations, a playful cosmic compatibility score, and an original quote. Or invite someone to enter their own name through a share link. Scores are a repeatable name-based game, not a claim about relationships, personalities, or destiny.

Made for small experiments

Compare two spellings, try a meaningful phrase, or share a pattern with a friend. Generation happens in your browser, with no signup, database, or upload required. The downloadable image includes the whole pattern and the text you entered.

Contact & feedback

This project is maintained through the Constellation App GitHub repository. For bugs, accessibility feedback, or questions, use the repository’s issue tracker when available or the maintainer’s GitHub profile to find an available contact route.

GitHub is an external service and may require an account. Public issues are visible to others: do not include private names, personal data, or sensitive screenshots.
