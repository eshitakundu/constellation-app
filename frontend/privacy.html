Privacy notice

Updated September 20, 2026. This notice describes the current static version of Your Constellation.

Text you enter

The constellation generator calculates your pattern in your browser. The site does not send your entered text to a generation API, create an account, or store a gallery. The application does not use cookies, local storage, analytics, or advertising scripts in this version.

Shared links and downloads

When you copy a share link, your text is included after the # character in the URL. Browsers do not send this fragment as part of the normal page request to the hosting server. Anyone who receives the full link can read the text and recreate the pattern. Links may also be retained by your browser history, clipboard, or the service through which you share them.

PNG images are generated in your browser and saved through your browser’s download system. They include the text you entered. Take care when sharing either links or images containing personal information.

Hosting and technical information

The service delivering this website may process technical request data such as your IP address, requested page, browser information, and request time for delivery, security, and diagnostics. This is separate from the browser-only constellation calculation. Hosting log retention and processing depend on the host and its configuration.

For deployments hosted on Cloudflare, see Cloudflare’s privacy policy. The application itself does not maintain a visitor database.

External links

The site links to GitHub for project information and feedback. Following an external link brings you to a service with its own privacy practices. Do not post private information in public project discussions.

Advertising

This version does not load Google AdSense or other advertising services. If advertising is introduced, this notice must be updated to identify the services, explain their data use and cookies, and describe the available consent and privacy choices before ads are enabled.

Questions

Use the project contact routes on the About & contact page for questions about this application. Avoid including sensitive information in public messages.
